The five days of Diwali

The festival runs for five days, in this order.

Day 1DhanterasBuying something new, often gold or kitchenware, to welcome prosperity.
Day 2Choti DiwaliLamps at dusk, remembering the victory over Narakasura.
Day 3Lakshmi PujaThe main night. Diyas, rangoli, prayers to Lakshmi and Ganesha, then fireworks.
Day 4Govardhan PujaFood offered in thanks. The new year for many communities.
Day 5Bhai DoojSisters pray for their brothers, and brothers bring gifts.
